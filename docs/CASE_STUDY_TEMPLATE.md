# Case-study template

The Trace page at `/work/trace` is the canonical template.

## Recommended length

Aim for 5–7 substantial sections. The reader should understand the project from a fast scroll, while deeper copy is available for someone who pauses.

## Section pattern

1. **Hero** — one-line project thesis, short setup, role/timeline/status metadata, one strong visual.
2. **Context** — what made the problem interesting.
3. **Behaviour / evidence** — what you learned about users or the system.
4. **Product bet** — the important model or hypothesis.
5. **Key decisions** — 3–5 choices that changed the product.
6. **Ship / signals** — real usage, business or operational evidence.
7. **What changed** — the strongest learning or reframed problem.

## What to avoid

- giant “Design Process” diagrams
- exhaustive wireframe dumps
- every meeting or workshop
- invented impact
- screenshots without a reason for being there
- ending with only “thank you for reading”

The ending should show how your thinking changed.
