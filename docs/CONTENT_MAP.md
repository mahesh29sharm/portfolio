## Homepage hierarchy rule

Every career chapter follows the same reading order:

**Company + years → role progression → chapter thesis → timeline tabs → one project story → learning**

The company name must be the strongest transition signal. A visitor should never wonder whether they are still reading the previous employer.

## Case-study story model

Keep case studies concise and decision-led:

**Context → Behaviour → Product bet → Key decisions → Shipped evidence → What changed**

A case study is not required to show every workshop, wireframe or design artifact. Include an artifact only when it helps explain a consequential decision.
