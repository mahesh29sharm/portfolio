Existing product model: Query ™ Response ™ Query ™ Response

That became the central product shift. Chat was an interaction mechanism. It shouldn't be the information architecture.
Research ™ design principles
I turned recurring research patterns into product principles before designing features.
Trust - Don't require blind confidence in AI.
Visibility - Make context and system state explicit.
Control - Let lawyers decide what the AI works with.
Traceability - Every important output should lead back to evidence.
Continuity - Legal work persists across hours and days.
Scale - The same model must survive 3 documents or 300.
These became the criteria used to evaluate design directions.

What actually changed
Research changed the product architecture, not just the interface.
Persistent sessions - A session represents ongoing legal work rather than disposable chat history.
Files + session together - Documents live with the matter and retain their organization.
Visible active context - Selected files appear directly inside the composer.
Continuity - Users can leave and return without reconstructing context, suggested follow-ups made session context more useful.
Progressive navigation - Low-usage capabilities moved under More, based partly on usage data.
Traceability - Response included internal citations that highlights the source material, files retained original hierarchy.
These became the criteria used to evaluate design directions.
